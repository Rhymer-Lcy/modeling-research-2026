# Q2 classic law: uncertainty and robustness

Generated by `scripts/q2_uncertainty.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Three diagnostics for the same five parameters. They answer different
questions and are reported side by side so that no single interval is
read as "the" uncertainty.

The fit data is 1176 checkpoints from 8 trajectories of a single model family, and
its loss column is consistent with a deterministic evaluation of a
published law (see `q2-classic-fit.md`). Both facts narrow every interval
below, and neither is a statement about how precisely real model scaling
is known.

The leave-one-trajectory-out range is **narrower** than the bootstrap
interval for 5 of 5 parameters. That is the expected direction and not a
contradiction: the bootstrap draws eight trajectories with replacement,
so a replicate can contain the same trajectory several times and omit
others entirely, which is a far harsher perturbation than removing
exactly one. The two answer different questions, and the leave-one-out
column is the one that answers "could a single trajectory be carrying
this result?".

It cannot. No single trajectory moves any parameter by more than
0.0815% of its estimate.

## Profile identifiability

Each parameter is fixed at a multiple of its estimate and the remaining
four are re-optimised. The value shown is the objective relative to its
minimum: 1.00 means the displaced parameter costs nothing and the data
cannot distinguish it, while a large value means the data pin it down.

Objective at the optimum: 1.69794e-06 (Huber on log L).

Every parameter has a sharply rising profile, so all five are
determined by this data. What that data *is* remains the limiting
factor: determining the parameters of a recovered generator is not
the same as measuring how real models scale.

## What is NOT available here

Leave-one-model-family-out cannot be computed on this table: every
trajectory in it belongs to the same family, so the family-level
resampling unit has exactly one level. Cross-family sensitivity is only
observable through the independent validation tables, not through a
refit of this one.
