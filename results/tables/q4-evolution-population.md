## Time convention

The canonical row time is the accepted T-009 `analysis_date`: the Epoch AI publication date where
one exists (C2 `pub_date`, else C4 `c4_pub_date`), otherwise the leaderboard submission date. Each
row keeps its `date_source`; a fallback date is a submission (evaluation) date and is never called
a publication date.

| Date source | Meaning | Panel rows | Primary-population rows |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `pub_date` | Epoch publication date via C2 (organizer fuzzy-matched) | 440 | 295 |
| `c4_pub_date` | Epoch publication date via the deterministic T-009 C4 link | 25 | 23 |
| `submission_date` | leaderboard submission (fallback proxy) | 4,021 | 1,067 |
| `none` | no date (excluded by R1) | 11 | 0 |

- Leaderboard submission dates span 2024-06-08 to 2025-03-13: the dense part of the panel is this window.
- Publication dates span 2020-10-20 to 2025-09-09, but cover only 465 panel rows.
- Where both exist (464 rows), submission minus publication: median 103 days, IQR 36 to 171 days; 22 publication dates fall after the submission.
- C2's fuzzy-matched publication date on a fine-tune or merge is typically the upstream base family's date: the 255 C2-dated rows in strata C and D carry only 35 distinct dates, and 200 of them share their date with at least nine other derivative rows (most common: 2024-09-19 x 116 (Alibaba); 2024-12-12 x 34 (Microsoft Research); 2023-03-13 x 19 (Stanford University)).

C3 is used as an auxiliary source only; none of its rows becomes a panel observation.

- rows: 4,599 = 4,573 Open LLM Leaderboard + 26 historical (papers / reports, 2019-2024)
- leaderboard rows whose `Year` equals the panel submission year: 100.0% of 4,573
- rows whose submission and publication years differ (224 with both known and differing): `Year` follows the submission year in 224, the publication year in 0
- duplicate model rows (distinct score vectors, per T-009): 79
- historical rows that are panel models: 0
- historical rows whose `Average` differs from the mean of their six dimensions by more than one point: 23 of 26 (max |delta| 42.33)
- historical rows with at least one dimension recorded as exactly 0: 23

C3's `Year` is the leaderboard submission year, not a release year, so C3 adds no independent
release clock.
Its historical rows are not on the leaderboard v2 scale (their `Average` is not the mean of their
dimensions and unmeasured dimensions are zero-filled), so they are not used as frontier observations.
