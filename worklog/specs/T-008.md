### Standing findings this stage must preserve

The principal fit table is described as observed training logs, but its loss
column is numerically consistent, to approximately the rounding quantum, with a
deterministic evaluation of a published law. The supported statement is
**"consistent with formula evaluation / generator recovery"**, never "proven
fabricated", "malicious", or "definitely synthetic".

The consequence is load-bearing and must not be eroded: fitting that table
validates the estimator, not the world. Empirical support for real-model
scaling rests on the independent observed tables, and the narrow intervals the
principal table produces are not a measure of how precisely real model scaling
is known.

### Acceptance criteria

The stage fails if any of the following is not true.

1. The quality-bearing tables are audited for structure and provenance
   **before** a quality law is fitted, and the audit states whether each
   contains independent empirical variation or is predominantly a recovery of a
   supplied generator. Tables of different provenance are never pooled, and a
   table that labels its own rows by provenance is kept stratified.
2. At least two uncertainty diagnostics complementary to the clustered
   bootstrap are reported for every classic parameter, and no single interval
   family is presented as "the" uncertainty. A parameter the data cannot
   identify is exposed as such.
3. The generalized law is implemented as a tested callable. Its three partial
   derivatives are negative throughout the valid region, each is checked
   against a finite difference, and the iso-loss substitution `dN/dQ` is
   negative — better quality must not appear to require more parameters.
4. Parameter saving and equivalent capacity are implemented as distinct
   quantities with the correct signs, and the equivalent-capacity feasibility
   boundary raises an explicit error rather than returning an unexplained NaN.
5. A deliberately invalid fixture is used at least once to demonstrate that the
   sign checks can fail.
6. Every claim separates estimator recovery, internal predictive fit, observed
   external validation, and extrapolation. A quality exponent identified from
   semi-synthetic tables is reported as recovering the supplied mechanism.
7. Large-model tables are role-audited before use; a table of model output is
   never used as fitting data and then reported as agreement.
8. No raw input file is modified, and the public-safety gate passes.

Completing every independent component does **not** make T-008 complete: the
generalized Q/p closure legitimately awaits IF1/IF2, and the task stays `wip`
until then.
