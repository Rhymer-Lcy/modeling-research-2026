### Acceptance criteria

The stage fails if any of the following is not true.

1. The branch is cut from accepted `main` and contains no content merged,
   cherry-picked or copied from a task branch.
2. A cross-question interface matrix exists covering IF1-IF4 with, per row:
   producer, interface, consumer, schema/semantic contract, unit/scale,
   provenance class, validity region, required uncertainty, acceptance gate,
   current readiness, and prohibited downstream interpretation.
3. An evidence-to-claim map exists covering Q1-Q4 and the cross-cutting
   classes, each claim carrying its minimum supporting artifact, mandatory
   caveat and one of `READY` / `PENDING REVIEW` / `BLOCKED` / `NOT STARTED`.
   Where evidence does not exist the entry reads
   `BLOCKED - DO NOT WRITE AS A RESULT`, never a placeholder number.
4. A five-level claim hierarchy (A direct, B model-conditional, C
   semi-synthetic calibration, D extrapolation, E unresolved/diagnostic) is
   defined, and every future quantitative claim is required to be classifiable
   into it. The hierarchy is not used to score people or tasks.
5. A figure and table inventory records, per item: ID, question, purpose,
   producer, source artifact, dependency, provenance class, whether uncertainty
   must be shown, manuscript destination and readiness. No speculative final
   figure is generated and no plot type is locked before its producing task is
   accepted.
6. A units, scales and direction audit exists for N, D, Loss, Q, p, benchmark
   scores, compute, time, context length, uncertainty types and the validity
   box, each with canonical representation, source contract, known conversion,
   downstream risk and acceptance check. An ambiguous convention is recorded as
   ambiguous rather than silently resolved.
7. Every manuscript section is classified as `STRUCTURAL PLACEHOLDER`,
   `SAFE GENERIC PROSE`, `ACCEPTED SCIENTIFIC CONTENT` or
   `PREMATURE / MUST NOT FINALIZE YET`, with an evidence owner and the trigger
   that makes it eligible for final writing.
8. Final acceptance gates G1-G12 are specified with owner, evidence, pass
   condition and current state.
9. The review package states explicitly that pre-integration readiness work
   exists but final T-012 remains dependency-blocked, and that no unreviewed
   scientific result was adopted.
10. Interface self-test, whitespace check and the public-safety gate pass; no
    absolute machine path, ignored dataset, or another member's path enters the
    commit.
