### Objective

Ingest the organizer's official 2026 manuscript-format sources, archive them as
local-only material with proven integrity, audit the repository's current LaTeX
implementation against those sources, and make only the corrections that the
official material actually requires.

T-014 is an infrastructure and manuscript-conformance task. It is **not** part
of the Problem-F scientific dependency graph and must not become a prerequisite
that blocks T-007, T-008 or T-009.

### Prerequisites

- The two organizer files are present locally and are the official 2026
  material: the textual manuscript-format specification (`.docx`) and the
  manuscript template (`.doc`).
- A working XeLaTeX build via `scripts/build_paper.ps1`, with the third-party
  document class provisioned under `paper/template/` by
  `scripts/setup_template.ps1`.
- `docs_local/` is git-ignored.

`scripts/build_paper.ps1` and `scripts/setup_template.ps1` may be changed only
if a real reproducibility defect requires it.

### Forbidden paths and safety constraints

- The organizer's original `.doc`/`.docx` must never be tracked, staged,
  converted in place, or redistributed through Git. They live only under the
  ignored local archive.
- The third-party document class under `paper/template/` stays ignored and
  unmodified. Do not fork it, and do not copy its source into a tracked
  location.
- No absolute machine path, drive letter, user directory, or provider account
  information may enter a tracked file.
- No real team identity may enter a tracked file.
- Do not change the ownership or status of T-007, T-008 or T-009.
- Do not weaken `scripts/check_public_safe.ps1` to obtain a pass.

### Source authority for format decisions

1. the organizer's 2026 textual manuscript-format specification;
2. the organizer's 2026 Word manuscript template, for structure and layout;
3. the repository's own tracked manuscript policy;
4. the third-party document class, as an implementation mechanism only.

The third-party class is not authoritative where it conflicts with items 1-2.
Where the textual specification and the Word template disagree, prefer an
explicit textual requirement for normative typography and pagination, use the
template for structural intent the text does not contradict, and record the
conflict rather than silently choosing the easier behaviour.

The canonical local sources are:

    docs_local/gmcm-2026/source/paper_format_specification.official.docx
    docs_local/gmcm-2026/source/paper_template.official.doc

### Acceptance criteria

The stage fails if any of the following is not true.

1. Both organizer originals are archived under the ignored local area with
   SHA-256 and byte size proven equal before and after the root copies were
   deleted, and neither original remains at the repository root.
2. `paper/FORMAT_2026.md` records, per requirement: the requirement, its
   official source, the current LaTeX behaviour, a status of compliant /
   non-compliant / ambiguous, the implementation decision, and the verification
   method.
3. The built PDF satisfies, by visual inspection of every page and not by text
   extraction: the abstract page is numbered 1; page numbers are centred in the
   footer; no page carries a running header; the body begins on the page
   immediately after the abstract page; body page numbering is consecutive; the
   competition edition shown on the cover is the 2026 edition; title and
   level-1 heading typography match the specification; body text is single
   spaced.
4. `scripts/build_paper.ps1 -Clean` exits 0 with no `Missing character`
   warning, no missing asset, and no reference broken by the format changes.
5. `scripts/check_public_safe.ps1` passes in both `PreCommit` and `PrePush`
   modes, and no organizer binary is staged.
6. A structural Q4 model section exists and is wired into the manuscript after
   Q3 and before the general validation and evaluation sections, without
   substantive Q4 conclusions.

A build that succeeds while still emitting a stale competition edition, a
running header, or a top-corner page number does **not** satisfy this stage.

### Implementation constraint

Prefer the pinned third-party class plus a small tracked 2026 compatibility
layer loaded after it, plus minimal wiring in `paper/main.tex`. Do not fork the
class. Do not update the pinned upstream revision merely to chase a moving
branch. A clean clone must still build reproducibly from the existing
template-provisioning mechanism plus the tracked overrides.
